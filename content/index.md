# Rangoric SRD

Rangoric's combined rules for a series of games: one version of each rule, starting from the 3.5 System Reference Document and adjusted from Pathfinder and other Open Game Content.

- Start at [[rangoric-srd/Rangoric SRD|Rangoric SRD]].
- Every change from the source rules is listed in the [[rangoric-srd/Changelog|Changelog]].
- The Open Game License, each source's Section 15 notice and the Product Identity declarations are in [[rangoric-srd/legal/Legal|Legal]].

The text lives in the [rangoric-srd](https://github.com/Rangoric/rangoric-srd) repository. This site rebuilds from it at most once an hour.
